Title: ABC Corp HR Policy
Department: HR
Security: internal

ABC Corp payroll policies describe monthly salary processing, payroll exception handling, and employee record validation.
Employee records are maintained in the HR-Employee_Records dataset. Each employee record can include EmployeeID, employee name, department, role, location, manager, joining date, employment status, and payroll eligibility.

Only authorized HR and payroll users should access employee-level records. General employees can access policy summaries but not individual employee records.
